# Sprinkler Spacing & Layout Guidelines (NFPA 13)

## 1. Maximum Spacing Between Sprinklers
- **Light Hazard / Ordinary Hazard:** Maximum distance between sprinklers on a branch line is typically 15 feet (4.6 meters), and maximum coverage area per sprinkler is 130 sq ft ($12.1 m^2$) for standard spray upright/pendent sprinklers.
- **Extended Coverage (EC):** Governed by specific manufacturer listings and NFPA 13 criteria for larger coverage areas.

## 2. Distance from Walls & Obstructions
- **Distance to Walls:** Maximum distance from a sprinkler to a wall or partition shall not exceed one-half of the allowable maximum distance between sprinklers (typically 7.5 feet). Minimum distance from walls is also regulated to ensure proper pattern overlap.
- **Obstructions to Discharge:** Strict rules apply regarding structural beams, ducts, and lights (the "Three Times Rule" or positioning deflectors at required distances below ceilings/obstructions).

## 3. Position Relative to Ceilings
- Deflectors of pendent and upright sprinklers shall be aligned parallel to ceilings, slopes, and roofs, positioned typically 1 to 12 inches below non-combustible ceilings depending on the sprinkler type.
