# Fire Pump Room & Riser Design Guidelines (AI Knowledge Base)

## 1. Fire Pump Systems (NFPA 20)
- **Main Fire Pumps:** Electric or Diesel driven pumps designed to provide required pressure and flow.
- **Jockey Pumps (Pressure Maintenance Pumps):** Used to maintain system pressure and prevent the main pump from starting due to minor pressure drops/leaks.
- **Controllers & Power Supply:** Dedicated emergency power supply and listed fire pump controllers.
- **Testing & Fittings:** Provision of flow meters, main relief valves, suction/discharge pressure gauges, and test headers.

## 2. Riser & Valve Assemblies
- **Control Valves:** OS&Y (Outside Screw and Yoke) or Butterfly valves with supervisory switches.
- **Check Valves:** Installed on system risers to prevent backflow.
- **Waterflow Alarm Switches:** Vane-type waterflow detectors for zone indication.
- **Main Drain & Inspector's Test:** Required for system testing, maintenance, and pressure relief.

## 3. Local Requirements (Tehran & Kish Codes)
- Adequate ventilation and lighting inside the fire pump room.
- Proper seismic bracing for pipes and equipment in accordance with local structural guidelines.
- Clearance around pumps and electrical panels for safe operation and maintenance.
