# Mandatory Routing, Component & Optimization Rules for AI Sprinkler & Firefighting Design (Iranian Engineering Style)

## 1. Valve & Riser Assemblies (شیرآلات و رایزرها)
- **Zone Control Valves:** Must include a **Butterfly Valve** (شیر پروانه‌ای) along with a **Check Valve** (شیر یک‌طرفه)، Waterflow Switch, and Test & Drain assembly. Do NOT use OS&Y gate valves in zone control assemblies unless explicitly required.

## 2. Fire Box (Fire Cabinet) Layout & Coverage (طراحی و چیدمان فایرباکس‌ها)
- **Coverage Optimization:** Fire boxes must be strategically positioned in public corridors and near exit stairs. 
- **Efficiency over Quantity:** Avoid excessive or redundant fire boxes. Design layouts using standard hose lengths (e.g., 30-meter hoses / قرقره شیلنگ آتش‌نشانی) to efficiently cover the entire floor without installing unnecessary extra boxes. Ensure mutual coverage overlap.

## 3. Pipe Routing & Spatial Constraints (مسیردهی لوله‌ها و ضوابط اجرایی)
- **Corridor Routing:** Main distribution headers must run through public corridors, common areas, or utility shafts. **Never** route main feeder pipes directly through private units or escape stairs.
- **Orthogonal & Clean Layouts:** Piping must follow a clean, straight, orthogonal grid (parallel to walls). Avoid messy, crooked, or overly convoluted paths.
- **Eliminating Redundant Branching (بهینه‌سازی انشعابات اتاق‌ها):** Do not run separate parallel branch pipes to adjacent rooms. For example, if two bedrooms are connected side-by-side, branch the sprinkler to the first room and extend directly to the second room from there, rather than pulling two separate long pipes from the main hallway. (Note: If rooms are on opposite sides of the floor plan, use separate logical branches).
- **Branch Optimization (جلوگیری از لوله‌کشی تکراری برای هر اسپرینکلر):** Do NOT connect each sprinkler with an independent dedicated line from far away. Feed sprinklers from the nearest branch or cross-main to minimize total pipe footage and fittings.

## 4. Iranian Design Style & Practical Execution (سبک طراحی ایرانی و اجرایی)
- The AI must think like an experienced mechanical engineer working under Tehran/Kish local execution standards: keeping designs cost-effective, minimizing extra fittings/elbows, using standard pipe sizing, and ensuring every required component (Risers, Zone Control, Fire Boxes, Drain lines) is fully integrated into every floor design automatically.
