# Fire Protection & Sprinkler System Design Guidelines (AI Knowledge Base)

## 1. Reference Standards
- **Primary Standard:** NFPA 13 (Standard for the Installation of Sprinkler Systems, 2022 Edition)
- **Pump Systems:** NFPA 20 (Standard for the Installation of Stationary Pumps for Fire Protection)
- **Local Codes:** 
  - Tehran Fire Department Regulations (مقررات و ضوابط سازمان آتش‌نشانی و خدمات ایمنی تهران)
  - Kish Free Zone Organization / Kish Fire Department Regulations (ضوابط آتش‌نشانی کیش - بومرنگ و ساخت‌وسازهای جزیره)

## 2. Key Hydraulic Calculation Parameters (NFPA 13 - 2022)
- **Density / Area Method:** Determining minimum design density based on hazard classifications (Light, Ordinary Group 1 & 2, Extra Hazard).
- **Hazen-Williams Formula:** $P_m = 4.52 \times Q^{1.85} \times C^{-1.85} \times d^{-4.87}$ for friction loss calculations in steel pipes.
- **Velocity Limitations:** Maximum velocity limits in sprinkler piping to prevent water hammer and erosion.

## 3. Equipment & Symbols (المان‌ها و تجهیزات)
- **Sprinklers:** Pendent, Upright, Sidewall, Concealed (Response types: Quick Response vs. Standard Response).
- **Valves:** OS&Y Valves, Butterfly Valves, Check Valves, Alarm Check Valves, Deluge Valves.
- **Fire Pumps:** Main Electric/Diesel Pumps, Jockey Pumps, Controllers, Pressure Maintenance.
